From Kashmir, now in Waterloo

I'm in my first year of Software Engineering at Waterloo, on the co-op stream. Before this, I spent most of school building things meant to outlast me: the school's first student clubs, a school-wide rulebook, its first Model UN, and a timetable used by 1,500 people. I'm now putting that same habit into software and design.

I grew up around my family's orchards in Kashmir, which is where a couple of my projects come from too. The leaf at the top of this page is a chinar, Kashmir's best-known tree and a near-twin of the maple.

I'm drawn to web and app design with a clean, minimal feel, to products that are genuinely useful, and to the craft of keeping software simple.

Right now it's in Waterloo and in Kashmir.
